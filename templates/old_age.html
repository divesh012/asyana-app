Old Age Home

An old age home in Belora offers free food and shelter to elderly individuals with no family support, helping them live with dignity and comfort in their later years.

Residents receive nutritious meals, healthcare check-ups, companionship, and a safe, caring community that treats every elder with the respect they deserve.
